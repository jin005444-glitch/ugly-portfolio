The longer version

My practice started with print and image-making: posters, packaging, editorial and illustration. Design school gave me the discipline; freelance work taught me how to listen, pitch and deliver.

Now I'm studying game development — learning Blender and Unreal Engine so I can carry the same visual language into 3D assets, interactive spaces and games. Two disciplines, one eye.

UGLY is a one-person practice — no agency, no handoffs. You work directly with me from the first sketch to the final file.
